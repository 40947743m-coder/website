Our Story

A Legacy of Faith & Learning

St. Teresa Kanyange Academy was founded in 1989, has grown from a small parish school into one of the region's most respected Catholic educational institutions.

For over six decades, we have remained faithful to our mission: to form students who excel academically, lead with integrity, and serve their communities with compassion.

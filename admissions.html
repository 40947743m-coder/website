Join Our Family

Begin Your St. Teresa Kanyange Journey

We welcome families of all backgrounds who share our commitment to faith-based education. Here's how to apply.

Inquire

Submit an inquiry form or schedule a campus tour to learn more about our programs and community.

Apply

Complete the online application along with required documents, teacher recommendations, and transcripts.

Assess

Students visit for a shadow day and complete an age-appropriate academic assessment and family interview.

Enroll

Receive your admission decision, accept your offer, and complete registration to join the St. Teresa family.

Tuition & Aid

An Investment in Their Future

We are committed to making a St. Teresa education accessible to every family. Over 40% of our students receive financial assistance.

High School

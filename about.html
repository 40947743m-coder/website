1989 Founded
Our History

Rooted in Faith, Growing in Excellence

St. Teresa Academy was founded in 1989 by the Sisters of St. Joseph with a simple but profound vision: to provide an exceptional education grounded in the Catholic faith.

What began as a small parish school with 47 students has blossomed into a thriving community of over 850 students, serving families across the region with a tradition of academic excellence and spiritual formation that spans more than six decades.

Our namesake, St. Teresa of Ávila, reminds us that education is not merely about filling the mind, but about setting hearts on fire with purpose and love.

Mission & Vision

What We Stand For

Our mission and core values guide everything we do, from the classroom to the playing field to the community we serve.

Our Mission

To educate the whole person — mind, body, and spirit — forming Catholic leaders who excel academically, lead with integrity, and serve with compassion.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of Catholic excellence, inspiring every student to discover their God-given gifts and use them to set the world ablaze with love.

Our Values

Faith, Excellence, Integrity, Service, Community, and Respect — the pillars that shape our characters and our choices every single day.

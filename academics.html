High School

College Preparatory Excellence

Our rigorous high school program prepares students for success at the nation's finest colleges and universities.

Honors & AP Courses

Over 15 Advanced Placement courses spanning sciences, mathematics, literature, history, and the arts.

Dual Enrollment

Earn college credits through partnerships with local universities while still in high school.

College Counseling

Dedicated counselors guide families through applications, financial aid, and scholarship opportunities.

STEM & Engineering

Robotics, computer science, engineering design, and biomedical science pathways.

Performing & Visual Arts

Theater, concert band, jazz ensemble, choir, studio art, and digital media production.

Athletics

18 varsity and junior varsity teams competing at championship levels across all seasons.
